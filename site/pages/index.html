Open CMT Gene Table

The genes linked to Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease (CMT) and related inherited neuropathies, with one row for each gene–disease link that ClinGen’s expert panel has rated: {{ROWS}} rows covering {{GENES}} genes. Each row gives the subtype names that go with that rating, its inheritance pattern, where the evidence stands, and links to GeneReviews, PanelApp and registered drug trials.

This page is the data home. For what the ratings mean, how names were matched and what the table leaves out, read the explained, footnoted version on cmt.blog. It is general information, not medical advice.
